## Chinese

引题  
以新加坡人，建修新加坡谱式。

主句  
源在祖籍，根在狮城。新加坡根缘文化学会推广通俗、易懂、易查的「相册家谱」，助家庭以南来落地的第一世为开族始点，记下自己的开族史。

三列  
1. 新加坡谱式 — 不混算中国祖谱世代，从迁入新加坡并落地生根的第一代起算。  
2. 八大招 — 封面、姓氏渊源、祖辈迁徙、籍贯文化、世系、童年、家肴、族人资料。  
3. 活着的谱 — 保存在会员谱坊，五至七年可再修；印刷成书为另项服务。

## English

Eyebrow  
A Singapore register, written by Singaporeans.

Lead  
Our source may lie in an ancestral place. Our roots are here. The Society teaches a plain, searchable Photo Genealogy: begin with the first generation that settled in Singapore, and write the founding history of your own family.

Three columns  
1. Singapore generation count — do not mix generations with a China-origin register.  
2. Eight Methods — cover, surname, migration, ancestral culture, lineage, childhood, family dishes, family register.  
3. A living book — saved in the members’ studio and revised every five to seven years. Printing is a separate service.

Membership  
S$99 a year includes one studio account for one family book. After payment the Society issues a member ID from R1001. No payment is taken on this website.

Publications

Photo Genealogy (2026), published by the Society.
